Add to your home screen

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Always open it from the same browser on the same phone — that's where your entries are saved.

Reminders, honestly

This app has no server, so it can only remind you while it's actually open in your browser tab — not when your phone is locked or the app is fully closed. Keep the tab open in the background, or check the Log tab's "Next doses" list when you open the app. Add-to-calendar-style push reminders would need a backend service; ask if you want that added later.

Privacy

Your entries are stored only in this browser on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server: nothing you log is sent anywhere. The only outside request is the page font, loaded from Google Fonts.

Not medical advice

Peptide Tracker is a personal logbook. It does not recommend doses or replace your prescriber, pharmacist or the product label. Talk to a health professional about side effects that worry you.

Share it
